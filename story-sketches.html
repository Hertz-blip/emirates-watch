TRENDING — UNVERIFIED

Early Concept Sketches Allegedly Leaked From Case File

Attributed to J. Kitzberger — Updated August 7, 2026

A set of sketch pages began circulating online over the weekend, appearing to show early studies of both the craft and the figure seen in the security footage, drawn side by side on the same sheet with measurement notes in the margins.

The level of anatomical and structural detail in the sketches has drawn attention from readers who argue it would be difficult to fake without direct access to reference material, though others have dismissed the pages as an elaborate hoax built from publicly available footage.

The artist has not been identified, and the origin of the case file the pages allegedly came from remains unconfirmed.
