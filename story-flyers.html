SAE DUBAI — CAMPUS

Flyers Appear Across SAE Dubai Overnight, Students Report

Flyer design traced to S. Schultz — Updated August 6, 2026

Students arriving at SAE Dubai on Thursday found flyers taped to noticeboards, lamp posts, and parked cars around campus, each bearing a grainy printed image and a short warning message. The flyers appeared just days after the craft was first photographed above the skyline, and several students say they were removed by campus staff within hours of sunrise.

"I photographed it before I even thought about it," said one student who found a flyer near the main entrance. "By the time I went back with better lighting it was already gone. Someone didn't want it staying up."

SAE Dubai has not responded to requests for comment on who was responsible for distributing the flyers, or whether their removal was connected in any way to the ongoing reports.
