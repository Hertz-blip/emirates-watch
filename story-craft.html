DUBAI — INVESTIGATIONS

Aviation Experts Baffled by Photos of Unidentified Craft Over Al Qudra

By Layla Haddad — Updated August 3, 2026

The photo was taken just after 5:40am by a jogger on the Al Qudra cycle track, who says the craft hung almost motionless above the trail for several seconds before angling away and disappearing behind the treeline. No sound was reported, and nothing was found on the ground afterward.

"I've spent twenty years working with aerospace design and I don't recognise this shape," said one aviation analyst who reviewed the photo on condition of anonymity. "It doesn't match anything in a commercial, military, or hobbyist drone database I have access to."

The image's metadata has been reviewed by two independent sources and shows no obvious signs of editing. No government body or private company has come forward to explain the sighting, and requests for comment sent to three separate agencies have gone unanswered.
