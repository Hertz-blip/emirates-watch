DUBAI — INVESTIGATIONS

Photos Allegedly Show Figure Captured Near Dubai Marina

By Omar Fadel — Updated August 4, 2026

The stills began circulating late Tuesday night, reportedly pulled from a private security camera overlooking a walkway near Dubai Marina. Their source has not been confirmed, and the property owner listed on the camera's registration has declined to comment.

"It moved wrong," said one resident who claims to have seen the original footage before it was taken down and re-uploaded elsewhere. "Not like an animal, not like a person either. It paused right at the edge of the frame like it knew the camera was there."

The Emirates Watch has not been able to independently verify the footage's authenticity or trace its original upload. Until an official source can confirm or deny its origin, readers should treat the images with appropriate caution.
