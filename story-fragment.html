Second Photo of the Figure Surfaces, Engineers Weigh In

Structural analysis credited to A. Mishelov — Updated August 5, 2026

A second photo of the figure was submitted anonymously to our newsroom this week, taken from a noticeably different angle than the original security camera stills. The submitter has not responded to follow-up messages, and the exact time and location of the shot remain unconfirmed.

"What stands out to me is the proportions," said A. Mishelov, a structural engineer who reviewed the image at our request. "Based on the scale you can estimate from the surrounding walls, the limb length and posture don't line up with anything I'd expect from a person in costume."

The photo's authenticity has not been independently verified, and no agency has offered an explanation for either image.
